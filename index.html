Systematic Literature Review Made Easy

This Free AI Literature Review Matrix Generator automates the process of extracting metadata from existing academic journals.

How does this AI Abstract Extractor work?

This tool is 100% serverless. When you upload a journal PDF, the PDF.js library extracts the text locally within your browser. The Groq API analyzes the metadata, and the final structured matrix is immediately downloaded to your computer as a CSV file.

How to open the downloaded file?

The tool generates a standard .csv file. You can double-click it to open it directly in Microsoft Excel, Apple Numbers, or import it into Google Sheets.
